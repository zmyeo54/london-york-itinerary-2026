Stamford stop · ~3½ hrs in town

Thursday · Stamford then York

Open drive Stamford map
Food & tips

Eat: High Street / St Mary’s Street cafés; try a pub lunch near the Meadows.

Note: Thursday is not Stamford’s main market day — still lovely for stone streets and coffee.

Fuel: Fill up before leaving London or near Stamford if prices look better.

Leave the car at the hotel

Friday · York on foot

Book Minster Minster map Market map
Tickets & hours

Minster sightseeing: Mon–Sat roughly 9:30–16:00 (confirm on the day). Book ahead for faster entry; tickets valid 12 months.

No Minster parking — walk/taxi from Railway House, or Park & Ride if needed.

Walls: close at dusk / bad weather. Do a short stretch near the Minster, not the full 3.4 km.

Market: often ~9:00–17:00; traders vary by day.

Food ideas

Stonegate / Swinegate cafés for coffee. Shambles Market for street food. Bettys (queue likely) or a quieter side-street bistro for lunch. Dinner near Micklegate / Holgate so the walk home is short.

If it rains

Minster first, then covered shops on Stonegate / Coppergate. Cut the walls walk. Market stalls under cover still work for a browse.

House morning · coast afternoon

Saturday · Castle Howard + coast

Saturday drive Castle Howard Tides (Whitby)
Visitor essentials

Castle Howard: grounds often 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00); house often 10:00–16:00 (last entry 15:00). Buy tickets online. Free parking (except Christmas period).

Robin Hood’s Bay: park at the top (Bank Top / Station car parks — RingGo locs 4640 / 4641). Steep walk down; shoes matter. Beach depends on tide.

Driving today: ~3 hours total — keep the house visit shorter if you want more coast time.
