Your London → York companion for 15–18 October. Plans, weather, maps, packing — plus a few practical notes before you go.

  • £Money: roughly £1 ≈ RM5.5–6 (check your bank). A £15 lunch ≈ RM85–90. Cards & contactless are everywhere.
  • 🌧Weather: autumn York is often ~10–16°C and damp. Pack a real jacket — it will feel cold compared with home.
  • 🍽Food: York Halal list is under Places (Three Chef’s, Middle Feast, Kapadokya…). Always ask at the counter. Stamford lunch → veggie/fish is safer.
  • 🔌Plugs & driving: UK Type G (same as home). Left-side driving. Road signs use miles / mph.
  • 🛂Entry: most visitors need a UK ETA approved before flying — screenshot the confirmation.

Book Minster Minster map Market map
Tickets & hours

Minster sightseeing: Mon–Sat roughly 9:30–16:00 (confirm on the day). Book ahead for faster entry; tickets valid 12 months.

No Minster parking — walk/taxi from Railway House, or Park & Ride if needed.

Walls: close at dusk / bad weather. Do a short stretch near the Minster, not the full 3.4 km.

Market: often ~9:00–17:00; traders vary by day.

Food ideas

Stonegate / Swinegate cafés for coffee. Shambles Market for street food. For Halal dinner see Places → Halal food (Three Chef’s on Gillygate, Marrakesh near the river, Kapadokya near the station). Holgate walk home is ~15–20 min from centre.

If it rains

Minster first, then covered shops on Stonegate / Coppergate. Cut the walls walk. Market stalls under cover still work for a browse.

Visitor essentials

Castle Howard: grounds often 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00); house often 10:00–16:00 (last entry 15:00). Buy tickets online. Free parking (except Christmas period).

Good to know

Money & payments

Think in RM: £10 ≈ RM55–60, £50 ≈ RM275–300. Contactless is normal. Some car parks need the RingGo app — set it up on Wi‑Fi before Saturday’s coast day.

Food

See Places → Food — filter by Halal, Must-try, Café, Stamford, or Near hotel. Always confirm Halal at the counter. Stamford midday: veggie or fish is safer.

Hotel breakfast is often eggs/toast/coffee — ask if sausages or bacon are pork. Many cafés do porridge, avocado toast, or eggs without pork.

Packing for cooler weather

UK autumn is not tropical rain at 30°C. Expect wind on the coast at Robin Hood’s Bay. Bring a windproof/rain jacket, warm jumper, closed walking shoes with grip, and proper socks.

Driving & signs

Left-side driving. Distances and limits are in miles / mph. York centre has narrow streets and cameras — leave the car at Railway House and walk on Friday.

Documents before you fly

Most visitors need a UK ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) plus a valid passport. Apply early and screenshot the approval. Keep hotel + car booking PDFs offline.

gov.uk ETA
Connectivity

Airport eSIM / UK tourist SIM is easiest. Download offline maps for York and the Saturday drive. WhatsApp works as usual on Wi‑Fi.

Local habits

Queues are serious. Pub tipping is modest (or round up). Tap water is safe. In the North, “tea” can mean a hot drink or an evening meal — context will tell you.
