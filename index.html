York Minster and the city walls

Friday morning focus

York Minster

The cathedral is York’s main landmark. Allow 1–1½ hours for the Gothic architecture and stained glass.

A short wall walk

Choose a section near the Minster. The full circuit is 3.4km — steps and uneven surfaces favour a shorter stretch.

Book Minster entry. Walls close at dusk and can close in poor weather.
York Minster, York

Robin Hood’s Bay

Saturday afternoon · Around 2¾ hours to explore

A fishing village by the sea

Red-roofed cottages and narrow lanes lead down to the shore. Enjoy the village, coastal views and a café stop. Beach time depends on the tide.

Parking and the hill

Park at the top. The walk down and back up is steep — pace yourself and wear comfortable shoes.

Robin Hood’s Bay, North Yorkshire
